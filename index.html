Our inspiration

Old symbols. New craftsmanship.

Celtic Forge celebrates the shared visual heritage of Scotland, Ireland and the wider Celtic world. This edition uses genuine open-source vector files from the Tabler Icons project, selected and renamed for heritage-themed laser projects.
